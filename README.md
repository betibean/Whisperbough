# Whisperbough

Hiii, and welcome to the orchard! (* ^ ω ^) ♥

**Whisperbough** is a cozy pixel-art orchard sim made in Unity, in the spirit of Stardew Valley. You inherit a secluded, abandoned forest garden where the fruit trees and plants have been left alone for far too long, and they really need someone to care for them. The atmosphere is soft, misty and intimate, more of a quiet morning in the woods than anything spooky. (๑˘︶˘๑)

> 🚧 **Work in progress!** This is an early-stage project, so things will change a lot along the way... (>ω<)ゞ

## What's it about?

Every plant in the garden has its own personality, moods and feelings. Your job is to slowly earn their trust, look after them, and bring the forest back to life. (づ｡◕‿‿◕｡)づ

### Tending the garden

- **Growth stages:** seedling → sapling → mature → ancient (◕‿◕✿)
- **Moods:** calm, angry, lonely, dreamy, blooming and more
- **Traits:** shy, playful, melancholic, sensitive... each one changes how a plant behaves
- **Seasonal preferences:** every plant has favourite (and not-so-favourite) seasons
- **Affection (0-100):** the more you care, the happier they are ♡(˶ᵔ ᵕ ᵔ˶)
- **Neglect:** forget a plant for too long and its affection drops, and its fruit may turn bitter, sour or even poisonous (；ω；)

### Soil

- Fertility, moisture, magic saturation and season affinity
- Watch out for overwatering and for harvesting the same spot too often! (・_・;)

### Daily weather & modifiers

Every day in the orchard brings its own little surprise! Soft rain is the cozy kind, and it boosts how much affection your plants gain from you. ( ˘▽˘)~♪ Heavy rain is a bit much though, since it can easily lead to overwatering. (；´Д｀) A warm wind helps everything grow a little faster. (๑˃ᴗ˂)ﻭ A sudden frost might leave some of your plants stunned, so keep an eye on the forecast. (>_<) ♥

### Ancient trees

Ancient trees give fewer fruits, but those fruits are powerful potion ingredients. They also hide secret recipes and can influence the plants growing nearby. (✧ω✧)

## The core loop

1. **Tend** to your trees: water, prune, fertilize and talk to them
2. **Observe** their moods
3. **Harvest** fruit, plant drops, scraps and seeds
4. **Brew** potions and **cook** meals for the orchard animals
5. **Fill up** your completion book with every plant, potion, animal and mood

### Harvest

Apples, raspberries, blueberries, plums, pine cones, dandelions, daisies, clover, carrots, sap, and more. (๑>ڡ<)☆

Fruits can have flavour modifiers: **Sweet**, **Sour**, **Tart**, **Spicy**, **Nutty** and **Magical**.

---

## Orchard animals

Friendly forest visitors drop by, and each one has its own favourite snacks. (=^･ω･^=) The hedgehog loves berries, plums and scraps/roots, and the fox goes for berries and apples. The crow likes nuts and berries, while the moth is all about nectar and sap. The deer is happiest with apples and carrots, and the rabbit will happily munch on carrots, apples and berries. (๑´ڡ`๑)

Each animal also has its own affection level, favourite fruits and seasonal activity, so there's always someone new to get to know. ♡

## Special events

- **Shooting stars:** a higher chance of magical fruit ✧*｡٩(ˊᗜˋ*)و✧*｡
- **Forest spirits:** animals might bring you little gifts (◍•ᴗ•◍)

## Getting started

1. Clone the repo:
```bash
   git clone https://github.com/betibean/whisperbough.git
```
2. Open the project folder with **Unity Hub**
3. Let Unity import everything (the first time can take a little while)
4. Open the main scene and press ▶️

> Unity version: 6000.4.0b7
